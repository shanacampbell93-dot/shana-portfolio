Prove you made it first.

Stamp a unique fingerprint of your design, photo, song or writing on the blockchain, with the date. If anyone copies it later, you can prove it was yours. Your file never leaves your device: only its fingerprint does.

Fingerprint (SHA-256):

The title is public and permanent on the blockchain. Don't include anything private.

Checking is free and needs no wallet.

How it works

What's a fingerprint?

Your device runs your file through a maths function called SHA-256, which turns it into a unique 64-character code. Change even one pixel or letter and the code changes completely. The code can't be turned back into your file, so nobody can see your work from it.

What goes on the blockchain?

Only three things: the fingerprint, your wallet address and the title you chose (if any), plus the time it was stamped. Never the file itself.

How do I prove it later?

Keep your original file safe and unchanged. Anyone can open "Check a file", choose that same file, and see who stamped it and when. You can also share your proof link.

Is this legal proof of copyright?

In the UK, copyright belongs to you automatically when you create something. This doesn't replace that, and it isn't legal advice. It's a dated, tamper-proof record that the file existed and who stamped it, which can support your claim. For disputes, speak to a solicitor.

Why the test network?

This version runs on Sepolia, Ethereum's practice network, so it's free and safe to try. You'll need MetaMask set to Sepolia with a little free test ETH to stamp. Checking needs nothing.
