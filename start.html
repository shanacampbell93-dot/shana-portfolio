Your first crypto wallet, with £0.

I'm Shana. Two days ago I didn't have a crypto wallet. This is exactly how I set one up safely and got free practice coins to learn with, without spending a penny. Follow along on your phone.

Step 1

Download a wallet app

A crypto wallet is an app that holds your keys: think of it as your account. I use MetaMask, one of the most popular.

  1. Open the App Store or Google Play and search MetaMask.
  2. Check the publisher is MetaMask (by Consensys) and it has millions of downloads.
  3. Install it.
Fake wallet apps exist. They look real and steal everything. Only download from the official app store, and double-check the name and publisher.

Step 2

Create your wallet and protect your 12 words

  1. Open MetaMask and choose Create a new wallet.
  2. Set a password for the app.
  3. MetaMask shows you a Secret Recovery Phrase: 12 words in order. Write them on paper.
  4. Store that paper somewhere safe and private.
The 12 words are the wallet. Anyone with them can take everything, forever. Never type them into a website, never send them in a message, never screenshot them, and never share them with anyone, including "support" staff. Real support will never ask.

Step 3

Switch to the practice network

Blockchains have test networks: practice copies where the coins are free and worth nothing. Ethereum's main one is called Sepolia. This is where you learn.

  1. In MetaMask, tap the network name or icon at the top.
  2. Look for Show test networks and switch it on. If you can't find it, go to Settings → Advanced → Show test networks.
  3. Choose Sepolia. If it's hiding, use the search bar and type "Sepolia". That's how I found it.
MetaMask moves its menus around between updates, so yours may look slightly different. That's normal.

Step 4

Copy your wallet address

  1. Tap Receive.
  2. Tap Copy address. It starts with 0x and is 42 characters long.

Your address is safe to share. It's like an account number: people can send to it, but nobody can take from it.

Step 5

Get free practice coins

A faucet is a website that gives out free test coins.

  1. Search "Google Cloud Sepolia faucet" and open Google's free faucet.
  2. Sign in with a Google account, paste your address and request test ETH.
  3. Wait a minute or two.
Some faucets ask you to hold real crypto first. Skip those. If one is empty, try again tomorrow or search "Sepolia PoW faucet". Never pay for test coins: they're meant to be free, and sellers are often scammers.

Step 7

Learn the screen that empties wallets

Sometimes a wallet asks you to approve a spending cap: permission for an app to spend your coins. When I was setting up, MetaMask showed me one for 2.2 million dollars by accident.

  • If you didn't go looking for it, don't approve it. Tap Cancel.
  • Read every pop-up before you tap. Scammers rely on people tapping fast.
  • Don't tap Buy while you're learning. That's real money.

You now know more than most people who own crypto.

You've got a safe wallet, free practice coins and the most important safety habit. Here's what you can do with your new wallet next, still free:

  • X-ray your wallet (switch to "Sepolia test network") and see your first transaction explained.
  • Stamp something you made on the blockchain to prove it's yours. No coding.
