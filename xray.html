Blockchain words, decoded

What's a wallet address?

A public ID, like a bank account number, that anyone can look up. It doesn't show a name unless the owner chose to link one (like name.eth) or explorers have labelled it. Holding the private key is what lets someone spend from it.

What's a smart contract?

A program that lives on the blockchain. It holds money and follows its code automatically: stablecoins, exchanges and staking all run on smart contracts. Nobody "logs in" to it; people send it transactions and it reacts.

What are fees (gas)?

Every action on Ethereum costs a small fee, paid in ETH to the network for processing it. Busy times cost more. Reading the blockchain, like this X-Ray does, is free.

What's a token?

A coin that lives on top of Ethereum, created by a smart contract. Stablecoins like USDT are tokens designed to stay worth about one US dollar.

Where does this data come from?

Straight from the public Ethereum blockchain, via the free Blockscout explorer. Pound values use a live ETH price from Coinbase and are approximate. Nothing you search is stored by this page.
